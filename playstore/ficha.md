## English

**App name** (28)

Lumi Gallery: photos & video

**Short description** (73)

A fast, private gallery: search in your own words, no ads and no account.

**Full description**

Lumi Gallery is a gallery that puts your photos first. It is fast, it tidies itself and it never asks for an account or shows ads.

SEARCH IN YOUR OWN WORDS
Type what you remember about the photo: "dog on the beach", "waterfall", "birthday". Lumi understands what is in the picture, reads the text that appears in it, and also searches by date, album and place. All the analysis happens on your phone.

A GALLERY THAT TIDIES ITSELF
• Pinch to go from a whole year to a single day.
• Repeated shots are stacked and Lumi suggests the best one.
• Automatic albums for trips and things: food, pets, nature, city.
• Memories from these same days in past years, with no notifications.

TRULY PRIVATE
• The app has no internet permission.
• Lock the app or single albums with your fingerprint, face, phone lock or a PIN of its own.
• An encrypted private folder for what you would rather not show.
• Share photos without the place where they were taken.

EDIT AND CREATE
• Crop, rotate, straighten and adjust light and colour. Filters, vignette and more.
• Draw or write on top of a photo.
• Remove the background and keep the person or object.
• Collages, and lossless video trimming.
• Copy the text that appears in any photo.

FREE UP SPACE WHEN YOU WANT
Lumi tells you how much you can recover from duplicates, repeated shots, old screenshots and large videos. You decide what goes; nothing is deleted on its own.

A FULL VIDEO PLAYER
Double tap to skip, playback speed, floating window, swipe for brightness and volume, and frame capture.

NO SMALL PRINT
No ads, no account and no subscription.
